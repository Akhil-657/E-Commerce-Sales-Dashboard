# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes e-commerce sales, profit, and customer behavior to show **what sells, where, and when the business makes (or loses) money**.

![E-Commerce Sales Dashboard](E-Commerce_Sales_Dashboard.png)

## 🎯 Objective
To track sales performance across states, categories, payment modes, and months, and to identify the products and periods that drive or hurt profit.

## 📌 Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales Amount | 438K |
| Total Profit | 37K |
| Total Quantity Sold | 5,615 |

## 🔍 Dashboard Highlights
- **Sum of Amount by State:** Maharashtra leads in sales, followed by Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi.
- **Sum of Quantity by Category:** Clothing makes up 63% of items sold, followed by Electronics (21%) and Furniture (17%).
- **Profit by Month:** Profit is strongest in January and November, with losses in May, July, September, and December.
- **Sum of Amount by Customer Name:** Top customers by purchase value are Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan, and Shiva.
- **Sum of Quantity by Payment Mode:** COD is the most used mode (44%), followed by UPI (21%), Debit Card (13%), Credit Card (12%), and EMI (10%).
- **Sum of Profit by Sub-Category:** Printers and Bookcases are the top profit contributors, followed by Saree, Accessories, and Tables.
- **Interactive Filters:** Quarter slicers (Qtr 1 to Qtr 4) and a dropdown filter to slice the whole dashboard.

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Clothing drives volume:** nearly two-thirds of all items sold.
- **Maharashtra is the top market** by sales amount.
- **Cash on Delivery (44%) is the most popular payment mode**, so there is room to push digital payments like UPI.
- **Profit is uneven across the year:** four months (May, July, September, December) show negative profit, which needs a closer look at discounts and costs.
- **Printers and Bookcases** are the most profitable sub-categories.
